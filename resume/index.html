Hands-on projects

Drone Flight Data Logger — 2026

  • Bench-tested the wiring and laid it out in a wiring diagram with a 15-wire pin-to-pin table (from, to, wire colour).
  • Soldered the wires straight to the boards, twisted the shared 5 V and ground wires into single joints, soldered them, and heat-shrunk them.
  • Secured the pressure sensor with hot glue inside a ~4 g 3D-printed mount I designed in SolidWorks.
  • Used a multimeter to troubleshoot power, then flew it on a DJI Mavic Mini to about 150 m and logged pressure, temperature, and altitude to a microSD card.

Temperature and Humidity Logging Experiment — 2026

  • Built an Arduino Uno and DHT11 temperature/humidity sensor logging experiment and wrote a test report.

Astraeus (AerospaceBot) — 2026

  • Built an AI aerospace engineering assistant that runs checked calculation programs (Hohmann transfers, orbital parameters, nozzle area–Mach, throat sizing, rocket motor performance, standard atmosphere, and V–n diagrams).
  • Published the source at github.com/Lukedw09/AerospaceBot and at astraeus.de-wet.com.

Moon Base Challenge (NASA, student project) — 2026

  • Made sunlight maps of the Connecting Ridge (CR1) at the lunar south pole at 10, 15, and 20 m above the surface for vertical solar masts.
  • Checked the 10 m map against published values (Gläser et al. 2014).

Laser Power-Beaming Demo — 2026 (in progress)

  • Building a solar-powered laser link to a photovoltaic receiver to measure end-to-end efficiency.
