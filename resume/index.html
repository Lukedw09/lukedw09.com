Astraeus (AerospaceBot) — 2026

  • I built an AI aerospace engineering assistant that runs checked calculation programs: Hohmann transfers, orbital parameters, nozzle area–Mach, throat sizing, rocket motor performance, standard atmosphere, and V–n diagrams.
  • The source is public at github.com/Lukedw09/AerospaceBot and at astraeus.de-wet.com.
