01 Live

Astraeus

Aerospace engineering assistant

Astraeus is an AI aerospace engineering assistant that runs checked calculation programs. The public source is on GitHub, and the same assistant is on the web at astraeus.de-wet.com.

  • Hohmann transfers
  • Orbital parameters
  • Nozzle area–Mach
  • Throat sizing
  • Motor performance
  • Standard atmosphere
  • V–n diagrams

astraeus.de-wet.com github.com/Lukedw09/AerospaceBot

02 Live

FlightLog

Flight-data analysis and simulation

FlightLog is a web tool for flight-data analysis and simulation. It takes a CSV from a launch, or simulates one, and draws elevation and pressure against time.

flightlog.lukedw09.com github.com/Lukedw09/FlightLog

03 In progress

Mavic Mini flight logger

On the aircraft

A Mavic Mini flight logger, still in progress. It uses an Arduino Nano, a BMP180 barometer, and a microSD card logging at 20 Hz, in a 3D-printed top mount designed in SolidWorks.

04 Live

StarbaseLaunchApp

Starship Flight 15

A launch-day companion for Starship Flight 15 at Starbase, Texas. It runs a countdown, shows the flight timeline, points toward the tower, and warns a few seconds before the sound arrives. It is an unofficial fan app and is not affiliated with SpaceX.

starbaselaunchapp.lukedw09.workers.dev github.com/Lukedw09/StarbaseLaunchApp

05 Live

SatPass

Satellite passes

A browser satellite-pass predictor. It shows when the ISS, Tiangong, Hubble, and other bright satellites cross the sky, and which of those passes can be seen with the naked eye. It opens on Killearn Lakes, north Tallahassee.

satpass.lukedw09.workers.dev github.com/Lukedw09/satpass
