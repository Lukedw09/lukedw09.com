01 Live

Astraeus

Aerospace engineering assistant

I built Astraeus, an AI aerospace engineering assistant that runs checked calculation programs. The public source is on GitHub, and I run the same assistant on the web at astraeus.de-wet.com.

  • Hohmann transfers
  • Orbital parameters
  • Nozzle area–Mach
  • Throat sizing
  • Motor performance
  • Standard atmosphere
  • V–n diagrams

astraeus.de-wet.com github.com/Lukedw09/AerospaceBot

FlightLog

Flight-data analysis and simulation

I built FlightLog, a web tool for flight-data analysis and simulation. I use it to take a CSV from a launch, or to simulate one, and it draws elevation and pressure against time.

flightlog.lukedw09.com github.com/Lukedw09/FlightLog

03 In progress

Mavic Mini flight logger

Bench test passed · flight test next

I built an Arduino Nano altitude logger for a DJI Mavic Mini. The bench test passed on November 12, 2026, and the flight test is next. I wired a 5 V boost converter, a BMP180 barometer over I2C, and microSD logging, and I used a multimeter to troubleshoot power. I designed a ~4 g 3D-printed top mount in SolidWorks.

05 Live

StarbaseLaunchApp

Starship Flight 15

I built a launch-day companion for Starship Flight 15 at Starbase, Texas. It runs a countdown, shows the flight timeline, points toward the tower, and warns a few seconds before the sound arrives. It is an unofficial fan app and is not affiliated with SpaceX.

starbaselaunchapp.lukedw09.com github.com/Lukedw09/StarbaseLaunchApp

06 Live

SatPass

Satellite passes

I built a browser satellite-pass predictor. It shows when the ISS, Tiangong, Hubble, and other bright satellites cross the sky, and which of those passes can be seen with the naked eye. It opens on Tallahassee.
